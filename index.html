FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking.

How does the remote session work?

We use a secure, encrypted screen-sharing tool — no installation needed on your end. You'll get a link, click it, and grant us view/control. You see everything we do and can disconnect anytime. Most sessions last 15–45 minutes.

What if you can't fix my issue?

If we can't resolve your issue, there's no charge. We'll also point you to the best next steps — whether that's an Apple Store appointment, a hardware repair shop, or a specific replacement part.

Do you support the latest OS versions?

Yes! We stay current with all public releases of iOS, iPadOS, and macOS — including the latest major versions. We also handle beta-version issues (with the understanding that some beta bugs are by-design).

Is my data safe during the session?

Absolutely. All connections are end-to-end encrypted. We never store screen recordings, and our experts are bound by a strict no-data-retention policy. You can revoke access at any time with one click.

What if I need help outside business hours?

Priority subscribers get after-hours access. Per-incident customers can submit a ticket anytime and we'll respond within 2 hours during our operating hours (8 AM – 10 PM ET, 7 days).

Can you fix hardware problems too?

We focus on software troubleshooting. If we determine the issue is hardware-related (dead battery, broken screen, failing SSD, etc.), we'll let you know and recommend the best repair option. No charge for the diagnosis.

How do I pay?

We accept all major credit cards and PayPal. Payment is processed securely after the fix is confirmed. For subscriptions, you're billed monthly and can cancel anytime.
